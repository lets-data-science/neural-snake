# Contributors

- **[Dev Suthar](https://github.com/devsutharsystems)** created Neural Snake: the engine, the data generator, the model and its training runs, the browser pages, the runtime self-test, the measured results in `eval/` and `evidence/` and his write-ups in `docs/`.
- **[LDS Team](https://github.com/lets-data-science)** built the Let's Data Science teaching edition: the three learner exercises in `learner/` with their tests and completed versions in `checkpoints/`, the commands in `scripts/` for data, training, export and restore, the small changes listed in `NOTICE`, the documentation and the step-by-step guide at https://letsdatascience.com/projects/neural-snake.

This repository starts from a credited import of Dev's release v1.1.1, followed by the LDS teaching edition. Provenance is recorded in `UPSTREAM.json`; redistribution terms and attribution are in `LICENSE`, `NOTICE` and `LICENSING.md`.
